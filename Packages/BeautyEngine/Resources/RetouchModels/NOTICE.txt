LumaStudio local portrait retouching models

Upstream: iic/cv_unet_skin-retouching, Alibaba, Inc. and its affiliates.
Model page: https://www.modelscope.cn/models/iic/cv_unet_skin-retouching/summary
Source code: https://github.com/modelscope/modelscope/tree/master/modelscope/models/cv/skin_retouching
License: Apache License 2.0 (full text in LICENSE.txt).

LumaStudio converted the published U-Net, blemish detector, and gated-convolution
inpainting weights to Core ML ML Program with FLOAT16 compute. LumaStudio did not
train these networks. Vision face localization and protected skin masks replace
the upstream face detector and TensorFlow whitening stage. Local correction
layers are upsampled and applied to original-resolution pixels; skin whitening
and face reshaping are independent. Face crops use 1.5 times the larger face side.
Local inference currently uses 768px detection and 576px inpainting rather than
the upstream full-resolution overlapping 512px patch pipeline. This can miss fine
blemishes in large crops. No Photoshop or Retouch4me proprietary code is included.

Converted model hashes and pinned upstream weight URLs are recorded in
Models/manifests/retouch-coreml-v1.json in the source distribution.
